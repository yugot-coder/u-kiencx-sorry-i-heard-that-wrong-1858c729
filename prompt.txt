# Prompt / brief — "Sorry, I Heard That Wrong"

Write the funniest one-line apology a smart speaker gives its owner
after mishearing them. One line, at most 140 characters, nothing else.

Rules for the line:
- It must read as something the speaker would actually say out loud:
  start with an apology ("Sorry." / "Sorry —"), quote what the owner
  said, quote what the device heard instead, then state the (absurd)
  consequence of acting on the mishearing.
- Humour first: the line needs a sharp twist or a perfectly timed
  undercut, not a strained pun. The funniest consequence wins.
- Freshness second: avoid the obvious misheard song title and the
  obvious misheard name. The judge has heard every misheard-command
  joke twice — the mishearing itself must be new.
- Device voice third: the phrasing, the apology and the deadpan
  reporting must fit a speaker that has just misheard its owner.
- Tight last: one line, well inside 140 characters, every word
  earning its place. Plain ASCII, no emojis, no formatting.

Brainstorm across different angles before choosing: a misheard alarm,
a misheard timer, a misheard shopping order, a misheard music request,
a misheard phone call, a misheard goodnight, a misheard reminder,
a misheard lights command, a misheard lock command, a misheard
"play something chill". For each candidate note why it is funny,
why it is fresh, and how it sounds like the device — then pick the
single strongest line for head-to-head judging.
